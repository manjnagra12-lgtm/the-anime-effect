About the project

Made for anime fans.

The Anime Effect brings anime discovery, personal watch tracking, and fan conversations together in one place.

Why it was developed

More than a list of shows

Anime fans often want to find something new, remember where they left off, and talk about what they are watching. This project was developed to bring those everyday activities together in a simple community app—and to put full-stack web development into practice with a responsive interface, user accounts, and connected features.

What you can do

Your fan toolkit

Discover anime

Browse the catalog, search by title, genre, or studio, and open a show to see its synopsis and details. Browsing is available without an account.

Track your watchlist

Sign in to add a show to your personal tracker. Set its status to Plan to Watch, Watching, or Completed, update your episode progress, and remove entries when you like.

Join the community

Read the community feed, publish your own posts, and comment on other fans’ posts. You can edit or delete your own posts; an account is needed to post or comment.

Getting started

How to use the app

  1. 01

    Explore

    Choose Discover to browse the catalog. Use the search box to narrow it by title, genre, or studio, then select a show for its details.

  2. 02

    Create an account

    To save progress or take part in the community, create an account or log in.

  3. 03

    Keep track

    On an anime detail page, choose “Add to my tracker”. Pick a status and episode number. Later, open “My tracker” in the navigation to update or remove it.

  4. 04

    Share your thoughts

    Open Community to read posts. Write a post to share a review or recommendation, or open a post to leave a comment.
